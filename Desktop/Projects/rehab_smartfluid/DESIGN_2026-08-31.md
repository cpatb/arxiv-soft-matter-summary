# Smart-fluid rehabilitation hardware: company design

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Status:** design approved in session, nothing built
**Owner:** author (soft matter, rheology, DNS). Solo through phase 0.
**Intended later:** colleague A (MechE MS, human biokinematics), colleague B (medical
student, likely orthopaedics). Neither has been approached.

---

## 1. What this is

A human-in-the-loop simulation platform for variable-impedance rehabilitation hardware
built on smart fluids. The platform answers two questions a device company currently
cannot: will this device do what we want on a real gait cycle, and what fluid should be
inside it.

Simulation and the design IP it produces are the product for the first 12-18 months.
Hardware is what the simulation is for, and what a raise at the end of that period pays
to build.

The platform is device-agnostic by architecture, but one device drives every validation
gate. Agnosticism here is a property that gets tested (§5), not an intention.

---

## 2. Solo first, and why the fluid layer is not optional

Phase 0 is built alone. Two collaborators are identified and neither has been approached,
deliberately: giving away a third of a company for advice is expensive, and phase 0 is
exactly the artifact that makes a recruiting conversation worth having. They come in when
there is something to show.

That decision has a cost, recorded here so it is not forgotten. The clinical collaborator
was the design's anchor against drift, and a device-agnostic platform built solo with no
clinical contact is the configuration most likely to become a very good simulator of
nothing in particular. Section 9 substitutes a forcing function for that.

On the physics: the company covers human movement, a clinical indication, and the
constitutive physics of dense suspensions. The first two are common in this field. The
third is not, and it is the only reason this is not competing head-on with established
biomechanics groups on their own ground. Drop the fluid layer and it becomes a generic
rehab-robotics startup run by a physicist, against people who have done it for twenty
years. Keep it and it owns a layer nobody else has.

## 3. Market check, verified 2026-08-31

Verified by web search in session. Sources listed. Re-check before quoting externally.

### Smart fluids are already the actuator in this field

Magnetorheological fluid prosthetic knees are commercial and heavily patented. The Össur
lineage includes US 6764520, RE42903, 7335233, 7279009, 7799091, and a recent patent on
resistive torque control in an MR actuator using a recovery pulse (US 12370066). Treat
MR-in-a-prosthetic-knee as occupied.

- https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/6764520
- https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10781202

### The shear-thickening equivalent is academic and uncommercialised

A 2020 *Smart Materials and Structures* paper built a variable-stiffness prosthetic foot
on a shear-thickening fluid and measured its critical shear rate at 10-80 s⁻¹, which sits
inside the gait band. STF-embedded foam wearables cut induced strain by 30% (foam) to 50%
(TPU skin). Neither is a product.

The critical shear rate is a design variable set by particle size and packing fraction.
Tuning where stiffening switches on, so that it coincides with a named gait event, is a
rheology problem that happens to be sold as hardware.

- https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1361-665X/ab9547
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12843172/

### The control literature for these devices is phenomenological

MR and STF damper control runs on Bouc-Wen and Bingham models: curve fits containing no
packing fraction, no particle size, no carrier viscosity. They do not extrapolate outside
the envelope they were fitted in, and they cannot answer a design question. This is the
gap the company occupies. See §6.

## 4. Alternatives considered and rejected

Recorded so they are not re-proposed.

**Injectable orthopaedic pastes (filter-pressing prediction).** The physics fits the
author closely. Injectability in that literature is defined as staying homogeneous during
injection independently of applied force, which is particle migration. Cement leakage
rates are large: vertebroplasty 54.7-87.5%, kyphoplasty 18.4-49.2%. Rejected here only
because it leaves A and B as decoration. Still a viable company for a different team.

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27838464/
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11745741/

**CSF shunts and hydrocephalus.** The problem is enormous and the mechanism is ours:
40% shunt failure in year one, roughly 50% at two years, up to 85% at fifteen, with
obstruction accounting for about 45% of failures, mostly tissue ingrowth and clots in the
ventricular catheter. A systematic review finds no valve design with superior survival.
That is suspension clogging. Rejected on team fit: it is neurosurgery, B is
orthopaedics, and the device is Class III.

- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12776210/
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1878875024020436

**Stents and thrombosis-as-jamming.** Shear-induced platelet aggregation runs near
10 000 s⁻¹ via von Willebrand factor entanglement. SIPA clots are 2.8x stiffer than
coagulation clots (3.8 vs 1.35 kPa elastic modulus), and SIPA is elevated in patients
with subacute stent thrombosis. Rejected because a multiscale in-silico SIPA model
already exists and the clinical domain is interventional cardiology, not orthopaedics.

- https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-62165-1
- https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021929021001299

**Standalone ASME V&V 40 credibility consulting.** Rejected on competition. Veryst,
Exponent and Thornton Tomasetti already hold this, the last with a dedicated biomechanics
and life sciences practice. A part-time founder does not win that pitch. The V&V 40
framing survives as an internal discipline rather than as an offering.

## 5. Architecture

Three layers. Only the middle one is ours to build.

### Layer 1: human

Musculoskeletal model and gait. Reuse OpenSim, MuJoCo or SCONE. Do not build this and do
not revalidate it. It supplies joint kinematics, joint moments, and the relative
velocities and shear rates seen at the device attachment.

### Layer 2: device

The smart-fluid element. A dense suspension in shear, valve or squeeze mode, returning
force or torque as a function of relative velocity and applied field. Physics-based
(packing fraction, particle size, carrier viscosity, frictional-contact transition)
rather than a hysteresis curve fit.

This reuses `Desktop\Projects\dst_fit` and `Desktop\Projects\tier0_wyart_cates_closure`
as the fitting and closure engine, so the monetisation lane carries over into this
company rather than being dropped for it.

### Layer 3: control

Semi-active control laws: clipped-optimal, skyhook, MPC. What makes this genuine control
theory rather than a slider is that a semi-active element is dissipative only. It cannot
inject energy. Controllers written for a bidirectional actuator fail quietly here, which
is one of the things the simulator exists to catch.

### The coupling is the hard part

The device changes gait, gait changes shear rate, shear rate changes device stiffness.
Evaluating the layers in sequence gets this wrong. Closing that loop is the technical
claim of the platform.

### Device-agnosticism as a testable property

Four interfaces, and only four:

| Interface | Carries |
|---|---|
| Human to device | relative displacement and velocity at the attachment, load path |
| Device to human | force or torque |
| Control to device | field or valve command |
| Device to control | observable state |

If these stay narrow, swapping between an ankle-foot orthosis, a knee brace and a hip
protector touches no code in layers 1 or 3. Phase 3 tests that claim by adding a second
device and checking it against the diff. It can fail.

## 6. Where the defensibility sits

Layer 1 is free and everyone has it. Layer 3 is textbook. Layer 2 is where the field uses
phenomenology.

A physics-based layer 2 produces a design map: given a target activation event, say heel
strike near 60 s⁻¹ and inside the measured 10-80 s⁻¹ band, which combination of packing
fraction, particle size and carrier viscosity delivers it? Device companies have that
question and cannot currently answer it. A Bouc-Wen model has no term for any of those
variables, so it cannot even be asked.

## 7. Validation

Published data only, for the whole programme, not just phase 0. This is a decision, not a
temporary state. It costs nothing, needs no rheometer, and is the reason the project fits
in 5-10 hrs/week.

Three separable pieces:

- Layer 2 alone, against published STF and MR flow curves and damper force-velocity data.
- Layer 1 alone: cite the existing OpenSim benchmark validation. Do not redo it.
- Coupled, against a published device result. The 30-50% STF strain reduction is a
  ready-made target with a number attached.

### The corpus, and what it can and cannot test

Checked 2026-08-31. The corpus supports the gate, but on two different axes that must not
be conflated.

**Material level, formulation axis.** Silica/PEG flow curves are published at 9, 12, 15,
17 and 20 wt%; separately over 10-30 wt%; and one study synthesised nine STFs crossing
two particle sizes (15 nm and 200 nm) with several weight fractions. The particle-size
axis is the more valuable of the two, because a frictional-contact model predicts its
effect and a hysteresis curve fit has no term for it.

**Device level, operating-condition axis only.** STF damper papers report force-velocity
hysteresis, and squeeze-mode work gives normal stress against mass fraction and squeeze
velocity (71 wt% silica: 38.48 kPa in squeeze against a 6.38 kPa peak in shear). MR
dampers are characterised across 0-2 A, 0.5-4 Hz and 1-2.5 mm. But device papers almost
always use a single formulation, so formulation extrapolation is not available at device
level. The coupled gate tests operating condition. Label it that way and do not let it
inherit the material gate's stronger claim.

Practical note: several MR datasets are available from the corresponding author on
request rather than openly. Corpus assembly includes emails and waiting, not only
downloads.

### The discriminator, and the parity problem in it

A Bouc-Wen model fitted to the same data matches in-envelope. In-envelope agreement
therefore proves nothing, in the specific sense this author has been caught by before: a
test that passes both candidates has discriminated nothing.

The obvious fix, extrapolate in packing fraction, has the mirror flaw and it nearly went
into this document unnoticed. Bouc-Wen has no packing-fraction term, so asking it to
predict an unseen formulation is not beating it, it is observing that it cannot enter.
A test one candidate cannot play is a definition rather than a comparison.

The gate that actually discriminates, in two parts:

1. **Parsimony at equal information.** Bouc-Wen is refitted per formulation, which it is
   entitled to do. The physics model is fitted once across the ladder. Compare held-out
   error against parameter count. The physics model has to win on parsimony, not on the
   opponent's absence.
2. **Cross-axis prediction.** Fit on the weight-fraction ladder, predict the particle-size
   change (15 nm against 200 nm). This is where a frictional-contact model has content and
   no curve fit does, and here the asymmetry is the point rather than a rigged comparison,
   because the physics model is making a falsifiable claim it can get wrong.

Report the candidate-separation ratio beside every verdict, and refuse to call the
comparison informative below a pre-registered separation. These two tests are the
platform's entire scientific claim, and both are allowed to fail.

## 8. Who does what, and when the other two arrive

Through phase 0 the author owns all three layers. Layers 1 and 3 are reused or textbook,
so this is tractable alone; layer 2 is the only one that needs building.

The recruiting trigger is a working phase 0: a validated layer 2 and a design map. Bring
them in against a specific gap rather than a general invitation.

| Person | Arrives | Owns from then |
|---|---|---|
| Colleague A (MechE) | Phase 1, when layer 1 coupling starts | Layer 1, device mechanism and geometry, bench mechanics |
| Colleague B (medicine) | Phase 2, when a device has to be chosen for real | Clinical use cases, which loading events matter, patient populations, gait-lab and patient access |

On equity: building phase 0 alone means the author is the founder and the other two join
later, on smaller stakes that vest. That is cleaner than a three-way split agreed before
anyone has done anything, and it is the main practical argument for going solo first.
Write the terms down before the first conversation, not during it.

## 9. Phasing

Budget is 5-10 hrs/week, solo, through phase 0 and most of phase 1.

**The forcing function, replacing the clinical collaborator.** The driving device is
fixed now and does not change: the shear-thickening variable-stiffness prosthetic foot of
the 2020 *Smart Materials and Structures* paper. It is the only published device with the
fluid, the geometry and the gait-band shear rates in one place, so it is a reproduction
target rather than an invention. The architecture stays general; the validation target
does not move. Reproducing someone else's published device is also the cheapest possible
answer to "what have you got to show", which is the point of phase 0.

| Phase | Months | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1-3 | Layer 2 standalone, published validation corpus assembled, reproduce the STF foot's measured critical shear rate |
| 1 | 4-9 | Coupled to layer 1, coupled validation on the same device. Approach colleague A |
| 2 | 10-15 | Control layer; the extrapolation gate runs. Approach colleague B; market conversations begin |
| 3 | 15-18 | Second device tests agnosticism; preprint; SBIR pre-application |

Funding posture: bootstrap through phase 2, then raise (SBIR or angel) once the
simulation has de-risked a specific device.

## 10. Kill criteria

Pre-registered. Each is a stop, not a prompt to widen a tolerance.

1. **Extrapolation.** If the physics-based layer 2 does not beat a per-formulation
   refitted Bouc-Wen on held-out error against parameter count, and does not predict the
   particle-size change it was not fitted on, there is no product. Stop. Both halves of
   §7's gate must pass; the parsimony half is the one that can be lost fairly.
2. **Agnosticism.** If adding device #2 requires editing layer 1 or layer 3, the
   architecture failed. Fix it or stop.
3. **Coupling.** If the coupled model needs per-trial refitting to match a published
   device result, the coupling is wrong.
4. **Market.** Ten conversations with orthopaedic rehabilitation clinicians and device
   firms. If nobody names variable impedance as something they want, stop and keep the
   paper.

   Going solo defers this gate to phase 2, roughly a year later than it should run,
   because it was the clinical collaborator's job. That is an accepted risk and not a
   solved one: up to fifteen months of work could go into something nobody wants. The
   mitigation is that phases 0 and 1 are reproduction and validation, so they produce a
   publishable result even if the market gate later fails.

## 11. Open questions

Neither of the first two blocks phase 0.

- **STF or MR first.** ~~STF is passive, unpatented and needs no electronics, but it
  removes the control layer. MR is controllable and more interesting to work on, but sits
  inside the Össur patent family.~~ **Answered 2026-09-01, and against the framing of the
  question.** `CONTROL_2026-09-01.md` derives the actuator bandwidth as
  `omega_c = gamma_dot / gamma_0`, which over the published gait band and the measured
  strain clock spans 0.99 to 25.5 Hz against a 10 Hz weight-acceptance demand and a 33 Hz
  stumble demand. Over most of the design space **the controller is slower than the event
  it is controlling**, and at rest the actuator has no authority at all, so it cannot be
  pre-positioned before an impact.

  So the passive STF route is not the compromise option, it is the honest reading of the
  bandwidth. Semi-active earns its electronics, power and patent exposure only in the
  high-rate, short-clock corner, and that corner is reached by choosing the material, not
  by writing a better controller. **Go STF-passive first.** The residual value of layer 3
  is as the thing that tells a customer where semi-active stops being worth buying, which
  is a saleable answer in its own right.
- **Freedom to operate.** A patent clearance read on the Össur family is required before
  anything knee-shaped is built. It is much cheaper to do now than after a prototype
  exists.
- **Where validation data comes from.** Resolved 2026-08-31: published data only, for the
  whole programme. The corpus was checked against the gate before this was written down
  and it supports both halves (§7). What it does not support is formulation extrapolation
  at device level, so that claim is not made. If a later phase needs bench data, that is a
  change of plan requiring its own justification, not a quiet extension of this one.
