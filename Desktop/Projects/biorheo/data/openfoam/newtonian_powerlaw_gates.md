## Two things the numbers say that the gates did not ask

**Both wrong maps separate by a CONSTANT, and neither constant is a property of the fit.** G11.5a is 1059 on every one of the 20 records, to 2.3e-13 — it is `rho - 1` = 1059 exactly, because forgetting `/rho` is a pure scaling. G11.5b is 9 on all 20, spread 7.1e-15, and it is `1/gammadot_min - 1` = 9, because the wrong exponent multiplies the law by `gd^-1` and the largest such factor sits at the bottom of the envelope. **That second attribution is not free:** the clamp sits a factor of 10 outside the envelope and the wrong map's value at `gammadot_min` lands on `nuMax`, so 9 could equally have been the clamp factor minus 1. Re-measured with the clamp 100x out it is 9 (spread 8.9e-15) -- unmoved, so the 9 is the envelope's and not the clamp's. So both discriminators test whether the slot is USED AT ALL; neither is sensitive to the fitted values, and a session that wanted a map error graded per-record would need a different candidate. That they are informative at all (both are far above 0.001) is what G11.5 claims, and no more.

**G11.6 refutes the reason NEXT_STEPS item 11 gave for refusing `structural_steady`.** The item said it is thixotropic and "has no counterpart there"; the steady flow curve equals `CrossPowerLaw` with n = 1 to 1.386e-16, so a counterpart exists exactly and refusing on that ground would refuse something OpenFOAM can do — item 6's mistake repeated. The refusal is kept and its reason replaced: `viscosityModel` is a function of the instantaneous `strainRate()` alone, so the STRUCTURE EQUATION cannot be carried, and the export would be right in steady state and silently wrong in every transient. `ThixotropyNotRepresentable` says that and gives the deliberate substitution.
