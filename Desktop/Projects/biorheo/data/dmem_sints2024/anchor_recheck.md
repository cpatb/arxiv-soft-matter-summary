## What no version of this anchor can show

1. **The fit method is shared.** For a power law, minimising rms `log(eta)` residuals
   is ordinary least squares of `log eta` on `log gd` -- the near-universal route to
   `(K, n)`, and the paper gives no sign of doing otherwise. Agreement certifies this
   repo's **parse, interval split and units**; it does not exercise the multi-start
   search or the profile likelihood, which this problem is too well conditioned to
   strain.
2. **The paper's own scatter is the size of the agreement.** Its figure captions and
   its Table 2 differ by 0.01-0.04 on the same conditions, and Table 2 is fitted to an
   ensemble of three samples, refills and different days while the deposit is one file
   per condition. The reference is not a point.
3. **The dataset's own noise floor exceeds the agreement on half the conditions.**
   Repeat intervals of the same condition move `n` by up to
   0.1044, larger than the deviations
   being called agreement there.
