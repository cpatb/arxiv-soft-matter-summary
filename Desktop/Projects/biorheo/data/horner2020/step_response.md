## Reading

The structural family's lam(t) at constant rate is a single exponential toward lam_ss (derivations/structural_kinetics.md, fact 3), so its stress after a step is monotone. Every human donor overshoots. The family is therefore refuted on shape by this dataset before any parameter is fitted; a thixotropic model for blood needs at least a second process (rouleau growth with a delay, or a viscoelastic contribution). The steady-sheet value at 1 1/s being a sample of this transient means the record's lowest-rate data are peak values from a rate step; the protocol itself is not stated in the workbook (see SOURCE.md) and is UNVERIFIED.
