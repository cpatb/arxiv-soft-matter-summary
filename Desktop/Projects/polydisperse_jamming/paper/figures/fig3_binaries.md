# fig3_binaries — the same number CV, two different packings

`φ` at `p̂ = 10⁻³`, `μ_p = 0.3`, N = 1500, mean ± sem over three seeds, for binary mixtures
at size ratio 3 (circles) and 2 (squares) against the VOLUME fraction of the small
species `X_s`. Both endpoints are the monodisperse pack. The two ringed points at ratio 3
have number CV 0.500000 (agreeing to 0e+00), the same size ratio, span and atom count,
and differ in which species carries the volume: `X_s = 0.0357` (1/28) is the member of the
number-symmetric bidisperse family of Fig. 1 at CV 0.5; `X_s = 0.25` is the same two
diameters with the fines carrying a quarter of the volume. They pack +0.0635 apart,
30.2 times the protocol's run-to-run scatter of 0.0021. The pair
holds the CV, size ratio and span fixed but NOT the skewness of the number distribution:
n_small = 0.500 gives skewness 0.000 and n_small = 0.900 gives
+2.667 (a two-point distribution cannot match both CV and skewness at two
compositions), so the pair separates composition from the width and span descriptors
only. The ratio-3 point at `X_s = 0.6` is omitted because it fails the
minimum-phase-count gate (36.1 expected large grains against a floor of 50).
