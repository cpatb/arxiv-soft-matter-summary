3. THE CHEAPEST REAL MEASUREMENT IN THE PROJECT, AND IT IS NOW HALF-DONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every rattler field on disk is a COUNT. The exact decomposition is phi = phi_B/(1-psi) with
psi the rattler VOLUME fraction, so a count cannot close it, and rattlers are preferentially
small so the two differ by a PSD-dependent factor -- precisely the dependence this project
is trying to measure. `measure.rattler_report` now takes an optional `volumes` and emits
`rattler_vol_frac_k*`; `packing.measure` supplies per-grain volumes for SPHERE packs only
(clump volumes are the analytic union and utils.porosity() double-counts the overlap).
Two bit-identity tests gate it (test_measure.py).

WHAT REMAINS: no cell on disk carries the field, because it is computed at run time. Decide
whether to re-run a grid for it. The geometry work's item B has a SHARP pre-registrable
prediction waiting: if backbone invariance holds, the required selectivity
S = (q - psi)/(q - Psi(q)) must land in 0.75-1.03 at every CV >= 0.3 across three families
and four rungs, and for bidisperse CV = 0.5 exactly 92.4 % of small grains and 7.3 % of
large grains must rattle. That is a real test, not a fit.
