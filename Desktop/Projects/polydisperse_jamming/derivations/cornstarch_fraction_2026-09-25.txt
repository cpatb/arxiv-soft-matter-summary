reading: the ball corner is De >> 1 AND S << 1. Fractionation moves De as a^-2 at most and leaves S
at O(1) (Hertz K_p is nearly size-free at fixed P_conf); a viscous solvent moves De but not S. Neither
reaches S << 1, which in this model needs a SOFT contact network (EDL-like K_p), i.e. surface chemistry.
