=== 5. CAVEATS THAT TRAVEL WITH EVERY NUMBER ABOVE ===
   (a) ⚠ POST-HOC. The two candidates and the resolving power were not written
       down before computing. See the module docstring.
   (b) ⚠ paper/main.tex: 'The frictionless ladders are not monotone in p_hat at
       this seed count, so this anchor is a check of the protocol's scale and
       not a result of the paper.' That caveat attaches to phi_0 here too.
   (c) ⚠ At CV = 0 the generator ignores the family label, so the two families'
       phi_0 values share a cell (found 2026-09-19). A test treating the two
       families as independent over-states the variance of their difference,
       i.e. errs CONSERVATIVELY.
   (d) ⚠ TWO families, not three. Bidisperse -- the non-monotone one -- has no
       frictionless block, so the family whose behaviour is least like the
       others is the one not tested.
   (e) ⚠ sigma* itself is untouched by any of this: it is a contact-force
       property and Wyart-Cates makes it independent of phi. Nothing here
       measures it.
