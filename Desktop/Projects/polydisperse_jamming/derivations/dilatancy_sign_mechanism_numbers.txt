=== SECTION 7: the ceiling on the dilatant pore pressure ===
  The binding ceiling is the NETWORK-MODULUS one of PORE_PHYSICS.md Eq. (4.5),
  p_dil_max = K_p dphi / phi -- a statement about how much dilation strain the
  packing HAS, not about what the fluid can survive. The cavitation cap that an
  earlier brief proposed was WITHDRAWN there (PORE_PHYSICS 4.4) for four reasons,
  the strongest being that it removes all particle-size dependence.
  uncapped Darcy, Eq. (4), ell = D0    = 4.651e+07 Pa
  network-modulus ceiling (4.5)        = 26.81 Pa [bracket 8.936 to 268.1 Pa over lambda_D 30 nm -> 1 nm]
  overshoot of the uncapped expression = 1.735e+06x  (SIX decades, not the ~460x a cavitation cap would give)
  withdrawn cavitation cap (1 atm)     = 1.013e+05 Pa  <-- NOT USED
  impact stress rho U0^2               = 1.296e+04 Pa
  ceiling / impact stress              = 0.002068
  ANCHOR-FREE RATIO (PORE_PHYSICS 9.4): p_dil_max/P_conf = (a/6 lambda_D)(dphi/phi)
  -- P_conf cancels, so this survives the withdrawal of si_fit's sigma*, which is
  the ONLY thing the absolute Pa figures above depend on.
    lambda_D =   30 nm (nominal 0 mM): p_dil_max/P_conf =    0.202  NOT available
    lambda_D =   10 nm (1 mM       ): p_dil_max/P_conf =    0.606  NOT available
    lambda_D =    1 nm (100 mM     ): p_dil_max/P_conf =    6.061  AVAILABLE as strengthening
  KEY STRUCTURAL POINT: the ceiling contains NO permeability. Where it binds --
  and for this system it binds by six decades -- the M1 channel is not merely
  sub-dominant, it is ABSENT, and the only PSD dependence left is dphi(CV) and
  whatever moment of the size distribution K_p reads (unmeasured; K_p ~ a for the
  double-layer contact, so it is a FIRST moment, not d32).
