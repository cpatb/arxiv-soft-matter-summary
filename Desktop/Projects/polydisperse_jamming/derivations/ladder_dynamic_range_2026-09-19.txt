=== S5  WHAT THIS DIAGNOSTIC CANNOT DO ===
   (a) SPAN/SD = 2 is a CONVENTION. Every group's value is printed above so a
       different bar can be applied; the ORDERING does not depend on it.
   (b) The sd is a 3-seed estimate, so it carries 2 dof and is itself noisy by
       roughly 40 %. A group at 1.5 and a group at 2.5 are not reliably apart.
   (c) It says an extrapolation is UNRELIABLE, not that phi_J is WRONG. A
       degenerate ladder whose four points happen to agree still brackets the
       jamming point; it just cannot locate it better than that bracket.
   (d) It does not replace the fit-quality gate the module already has. A ladder
       can have good span and still be non-monotone from a single bad rung.
