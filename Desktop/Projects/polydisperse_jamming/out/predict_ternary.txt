=== the NON-ADDITIVE alternative: SEQUENTIAL (nested-binary) filling ===
   The additive model sums an interaction over every pair. The oldest
   alternative in the powder literature is SEQUENTIAL: the two coarsest classes
   pack first, and the finest class then fills the voids of that COMPOSITE as
   if it were one medium. It uses the SAME measured binary closure, adds NO free
   parameter, and reduces EXACTLY to the binary closure at K = 2 -- so the
   binary calibration cannot tell the two apart, which is precisely why a
   ternary is the cheapest test that can.
   It needs ONE convention: what diameter the composite presents to the fines.
   That convention is a knob, so BOTH defensible choices are computed and the
   test is declared not informative if they bracket the additive prediction.
