=== WHAT CV DOES, and the two routes are not comparable ===
  Route 1, through d32: CV moves the SAMPLE along the size axis, because
  Carman-Kozeny's length is the Sauter mean and d32/d10 = (1+CV^2)^2 for a
  lognormal at fixed NUMBER mean.
  ⚠ PROVENANCE UNSTATED: whether the lab's '800 nm' is a number mean at all
  is not on record. psd.py notes laser diffraction reports d43; if 800 nm is
  a volume or intensity mean then d32 FALLS as CV rises and this route
  reverses sign. Q1 on the Status card.

  Route 2, through phi_c: CV raises phi_c, which shrinks dphi, which moves
  a* DOWN (a* ~ sqrt(dphi)). v1's SCALINGS said G1 depends on CV 'through
  d32 only', which contradicted this project's own measured +0.062 shift.
