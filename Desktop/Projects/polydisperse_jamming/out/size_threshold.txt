=== THE HYPOTHESIS SHARPENED: a WINDOW in size, not a threshold ===
  T1 and T3 both grow with particle size and they are NOT redundant, because
  they grow at different rates and point opposite ways:
     T3  strengthening capacity   ~ a^1   below it dilatancy CANNOT strengthen
     T1  drainage                 ~ a^2   above it the pore fluid keeps up
  so frustrated dilation can hold a drop solid only in the band between them:

  🔑 THREE things fall out, and each is checkable on an axis already in use.
  (a) CORNSTARCH sits INSIDE the window at 0.5 m/s and EXITS THE TOP by 3 m/s.
      That IS [M26]'s re-entrant transition, recovered as a window edge moving
      down past a fixed particle size as U0 rises.
  (b) 800 nm SILICA sits BELOW the window at 0 and 1 mM -- too small for
      frustrated dilation to raise the particle pressure above what already
      drives it. So silica's solid-like branch, which is OBSERVED, is NOT
      frustrated dilation; it has to be frictional/repulsive jamming. ⚠ That
      is a substantive claim about the lab's own material and it says silica
      and cornstarch are solid for DIFFERENT REASONS.
  (c) Adding salt lowers the bottom edge past 800 nm, so the SAME silica should
      enter the window inside the salt series Paper 1 already runs -- no new
      particles, no new apparatus.

=== WHAT CV DOES TO THE WINDOW: it closes from BOTH ends ===
  Both edges are functions of dphi = phi - phi_c, and CV moves phi_c UP, so CV
  shrinks dphi. The two edges then move TOWARD each other, because:
     lower edge T3 = 6 lambda_D phi / dphi        ~ dphi^-1   RISES as dphi falls
     upper edge T1                                ~ dphi^+1/2 FALLS as dphi falls
  so there is a CV at which the window CLOSES and no particle size can be held
  solid by frustrated dilation at all.

  What is NOT provisional is the SIGN of each edge's response, which is algebra.

  🔑 AND THE TWO CV ROUTES ARE NOT COMPARABLE IN STRENGTH.
     Within this same size-threshold framework CV can act two ways, and they
     are separated by more than an order of magnitude:
        through d32   (moving the SAMPLE across a fixed edge): CV = 1.45
        through phi_c (moving the EDGES onto each other)     : CV = 0.057
     a ratio of 25x. So even inside the author's own
     framing, the dominant CV channel is the PACKING one, not the permeability
     one -- the same ordering the dilatancy-sign derivation reaches from the
     two terms of d ln(ID)/dCV, and reached here by a different route.

  🔑 CONSISTENCY with the sibling mechanism: there, CV raises phi_c until
     phi - phi_c changes SIGN and the pack turns contractive. Here, CV shrinks
     phi - phi_c until the size window closes. Two framings of one shrinking
     gap, both ending the solid-like branch at a critical WIDTH -- so the size
     hypothesis and the phi_c mechanism are not rivals, they are the same
     statement read on two different axes.

=== CAN A PURE SIZE THRESHOLD EXPLAIN THE CV OBSERVATION? ===
  On a pure size threshold, CV acts ONLY by moving the moment the threshold
  uses. T1 uses Carman-Kozeny, whose length is the SAUTER mean, and for a
  lognormal at fixed number mean d32/d10 = (1 + CV^2)^2. So crossing a* from a
  starting d10 needs a specific CV -- which is a testable number, not a story.
   d10 [um]   a* [um]  d32/d10 needed  CV needed
       0.80      7.66            9.58       1.45   (T1, ID_b=6.6e-3)
       1.50      7.66            5.11       1.12   (T1, ID_b=6.6e-3)
       3.00      7.66            2.55       0.77   (T1, ID_b=6.6e-3)

  🔑 At the lab's 800 nm number-mean a pure size threshold needs CV ~ 1.5 to
     reach a*, and CV ~ 1.1 even at a 1.5 um mean. Those are far broader than
     the CV 0.2-0.5 this project has simulated. SO: if the author's two samples
     have CV below ~1, a pure size threshold acting through d32 CANNOT carry
     them across T1, and something else must be moving as well. If they are
     broader than that, it can -- which makes the raw size distributions the
     measurement that decides it (Q1 on the Status card, still open).
