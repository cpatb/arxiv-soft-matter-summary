=== 2. WHY: the model has an escape branch at b = 1 where phi = beta exactly ===
   The closure is a MIN over branches. The fine-class branch carries
   tot = (1 - beta)(1 - b) y_L, so at b = 1 it gives tot = 0 and phi = beta for
   EVERY composition and EVERY a. If the measured phi never rises far enough
   above beta, that constant model is inside the bar and a is free.

      problem and NOT a sample-size problem: it is the filling SIGNAL, phi -
      beta, falling to the size of the bar.

=== 3. does adding points or seeds narrow the interval? NO, and by construction ===
   The bar is delta chi2 = 1 PER POINT (mixture_closure.profile_interval:
   `prof <= lo + len(pts)`). Duplicating a point set doubles chi2 AND the bar,
   so the interval cannot move. Demonstrated rather than argued:
   r = 1.5: n =  8  w(a) = 0.0400  w(b) = 0.0850
   r = 1.5: n = 16  w(a) = 0.0400  w(b) = 0.0850   (same points, twice)
   r = 1.5: n = 32  w(a) = 0.0400  w(b) = 0.0850   (same points, four times)
   -> widths IDENTICAL: True. **More compositions at the same sensitivity
      buy nothing under this rule, and more SEEDS buy less than that -- seeds
      move the seed-mean phi, not the curvature, and the bar is in SCALE units,
      not in units of the seed error.** Any design that hopes to shrink these
      intervals has to move the SENSITIVITY, i.e. WHERE in composition it
      samples, not HOW MUCH.

=== 6. the ratio the K-ladder actually needs is NOT 1.25 ===
   K = 5's four pairs sit at u = 0.70711, i.e. r = 4^(1/4) = 1.41421, NOT at
   u = 0.8. The ceiling above is identifiable at r = 1.41421 on the OLD design
   and fails only at r = 1.25, where the filling signal is weakest. So the
   design question is not 'how do we rescue u = 0.8' but 'does the standard
   block work at u = 0.7071' -- and the ceiling says yes, AT the shipped a(u).
   Since the shipped a(u*) is the extrapolation the block exists to replace,
   the honest next question is how far it can be wrong before the answer flips.

=== 7. what the r = 1.25 data DOES constrain -- including the COMBINATION ===
   If a(u) is unidentifiable there, the fallback is to constrain the combination
   instead. That needs the JOINT region, not the two marginals, because two wide
   marginals can still enclose a narrow diagonal.
   n = 8 compositions; chi2_min = 1.36, bar = chi2_min + n = 9.36
   admissible area: 4.5% of the unit square (1811 of 40401 grid nodes)
   a range inside the joint region: [0.0001, 0.9999]
   b range inside the joint region: [0.0001, 0.9999]
   is the CONSTANT model (a = 1 or b = 1, i.e. phi = beta) admissible? YES
   -> that is the whole verdict. A pack indistinguishable from monodisperse
      constrains NO combination of a and b, because every combination that
      makes the binding branch vanish fits it equally well. u = 0.8 is
      uninformative about the combination, not only about a alone, so the
      monotone bracket a(0.667) <= a(0.7071) <= a(0.8) gains nothing at its
      upper end either.

=== what this does NOT decide ===
   * The ceiling is computed AT the shipped interpolated a(u), b(u) -- the very
     numbers the block exists to measure. If the truth at u = 0.7 is closer to 1
     than the PCHIP says, the signal is smaller and the real block does worse
     than its ceiling; if it is further from 1, better. The ceiling is a
     DESIGN comparison at a stated working point, not a prediction of the answer.
   * It says nothing about whether the model's FORM is right at high u.
   * MIN_PHASE_COUNT is applied per composition at N = 1500; compositions
     refused above would need N = 3000 and are not silently included.
