   The 2026-08-31 exclusion swept the FIRST term only, with phi - phi_c pinned
   at 0.02. Both are now computed from the same measurement.

=== which channel wins is a property of Delta phi, and here is the boundary ===
   Setting phi_c'(CV)/Delta phi = 8 CV/(1 + CV^2) gives the distance from
   critical inside which polydispersity acts through PACKING and outside which
   it acts through PERMEABILITY:
      cv     phi_c'(cv)   Delta phi* (crossover)    phi* = phi_c(cv) + Delta phi*
      0.05    0.04092        0.1025                0.6952
      0.10    0.05755        0.0727                0.6678
      0.20    0.07623        0.0496                0.6515
      0.35    0.14477        0.0580                0.6768
      0.50    0.19386        0.0606                0.7050
   -> the crossover DIVERGES as CV -> 0 (the permeability term is O(CV) and
      the packing term is O(1)), so near a monodisperse pack the packing
      channel wins at any distance from critical. At CV = 0.5 it wins only
      within about 0.05 of the critical line -- which is the same width as
      the switchable band itself, and that coincidence is not an accident:
      both are set by phi_c'(CV).

=== WHAT IS AND IS NOT SETTLED ===
   SETTLED: the switchable band is 0.0533 +- 0.0018 wide in phi,
     measured by a route that takes no input from phi_J, and the switch is
     STEEPER at high CV than a single slope implies.
   SETTLED: WHICH channel of Eq. (11) dominates is set by the distance from
     critical, and the boundary is now computed rather than assumed. At the
     derivation's pinned Delta phi = 0.02 the critical-state channel wins by
     2.5-5.1x; by phi = 0.80 the permeability channel wins by 2.5-4x. The
     order-counting (packing O(1), permeability O(CV)) is confirmed near
     CV = 0 and is NOT a statement about the whole ladder.
   NOT SETTLED: where phi actually sits during an impact. The sign turns on
     phi - phi_c AT THE CONTACT, and the bench phi = 0.44 is far below every
     phi_c here, so the band is reached only if the impact densifies the
     suspension into it. That is the drop-side measurement, not this one.
   NOT SETTLED: the absolute level of phi_c for a real material. Only the
     shift transfers from a frictional-sphere DEM to an experiment.
   NOT SETTLED: whether CV acts on the TRANSIENT as well as on the critical
     line -- that is the strain-clock campaign, PREREG_STRAINCLOCK_2026-09-13.
