=== 0. the model's own limits, computed rather than asserted ===
   K = 1 returns beta; ratio -> 1 returns beta at every composition; and
   ratio -> inf reproduces the closed-form Furnas branches at two different
   exponent pairs, so the ideal limit is a property of the RATIO, not a
   limit of the fitted exponents. ALL LIMITS PASS.
