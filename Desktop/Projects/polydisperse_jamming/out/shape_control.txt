=== the quantitative test: proportionality, one free constant ===
   If dphi_J = K * X, then dphi_J / X must be the SAME for all three families.
   variable          lognormal    uniform  bidispers     max/min
   cv                   0.1252     0.0485     0.0278       4.51x
   d32                  0.0402     0.0173     0.0099       4.06x
   skew                 0.0405    -9.5007        nan        nanx
   ratio                0.0025     0.0017     0.0046       2.66x
   d95_d05              0.0131     0.0030     0.0046       4.37x
   f_small              1.9949     0.1491        nan        nanx
   m3_over_m1c          0.0323     0.0138     0.0079       4.07x
   -> a collapsing variable would show max/min near 1. NONE of these does.
      The two that come closest are reported, but 'closest' among failures is
      not a result: what this establishes is that the shift is NOT a function
      of any ONE of these descriptors.

=== WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES, AND WHAT IT COSTS THE PROGRAMME ===
   ESTABLISHED: at matched CV and matched friction the CV 0 -> 0.5 jamming
     shift runs +0.0139 to +0.0621 across three size
     distributions, a factor 4.5. CV is NOT the
     control parameter, and every result this project quotes as a derivative
     with respect to CV is conditional on the LOGNORMAL family.
   ESTABLISHED: it is not a friction effect -- the ordering is identical at
     mu_p = 0 and 0.3.
   NOT ESTABLISHED: what the control parameter IS. None of the seven
     single-variable candidates collapses the three families, and with three
     families there is not enough leverage to fit a two-variable form.
   WHAT IT COSTS: nothing, IF the target material is lognormal -- milled and
     precipitated powders approximately are, and the lab's silica and
     cornstarch are both. But that is now a STATED CONDITION of the headline
     rather than an unexamined assumption, and it is a far stronger condition
     than the jamming-point matching transfer_law.py was built to check.
   WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT: a fourth and fifth family chosen to SEPARATE the
     candidates rather than to add coverage -- e.g. a negatively-skewed
     distribution at the same CV and d32, and a bidisperse at a size ratio
     large enough to fill interstices (>= 1/0.414 = 2.4), which the CV = 0.5
     bidisperse already is, so the interstitial picture predicts it should
     pack WELL and it packs worst of the three.
