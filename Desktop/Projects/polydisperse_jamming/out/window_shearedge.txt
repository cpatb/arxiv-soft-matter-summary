=== Q1  does the SIGN of dW/dCV survive the change of edge? ===
   A: dW/dCV = -0.00348 -> the window narrows with CV
   B: dW/dCV = +0.01040 -> the window widens with CV
   C: dW/dCV = +0.00949 -> the window widens with CV
   D: dW/dCV = -0.00102 -> the window narrows with CV
   -> the sign REVERSES between the shipped edge and the shear-measured one.

   ⚠ TWO statistics, and they do not agree on the grouping. The WLS slope
     weights by 1/se^2 and on these three levels that is nearly a fit through
     CV 0 and 0.2 alone; the endpoint difference uses the axis end to end.
      reading   dW/dCV (WLS)      dW endpoint      signs agree?
      A        -0.00348         -0.0040          yes
      B        +0.01040         +0.0047          yes
      C        +0.00949         +0.0023          yes
      D        -0.00102         +0.0018          NO
   ⚠ ALL FOUR READINGS SHARE THE UPPER EDGE phi_0, so any grouping among them
     is a statement about the LOWER edge alone.
     On the ENDPOINT statistic the three high-friction/sheared edges are ALL POSITIVE (B +0.0047, C +0.0023, D +0.0018) against A -0.0040.
     On the WLS slope D is -0.00102, i.e. the SAME sign as A and consistent with zero (t = -0.19), so that grouping does NOT survive the change of statistic.
     A claim that 'saturated and sheared edges move together' is therefore NOT
     supported: what is supported is that no edge resolves a width trend, and
     that the sign of the central value is set by the edge AND the statistic.

=== Q2  is any of them RESOLVED against its own error? ===
   A: |dW| = 0.0040 against 2 se = 0.0072  -> NOT RESOLVED
   B: |dW| = 0.0047 against 2 se = 0.0066  -> NOT RESOLVED
   C: |dW| = 0.0023 against 2 se = 0.0066  -> NOT RESOLVED
   D: |dW| = 0.0018 against 2 se = 0.0094  -> NOT RESOLVED
   ⚠ reading A's error here is NOT dst_window.py's: that one pairs the two
     arms per SEED, which cancels the shared realisation variance and is the
     right error for two ladders sharing seeds. B and C cannot be paired --
     the two edges come from different campaigns, N and protocol -- so their
     larger error is a property of the comparison, not a worse analysis.
   ⚠ phi_0(CV 0.5) carries se 0.0027 on a ladder of 3 rungs (dst_window flags <= 3 as THIN), and it is what dominates every error above.

=== WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE ===
   SETTLED: the SIGN of the width trend is a property of which lower edge
     is used. Central values: A narrows, B and C widen. And on the three CV
     levels the shear campaign covers, NONE of the three readings resolves a
     change at 2 se -- including A. dst_window.py's 'RESOLVED as a CHANGE'
     rests on SIX levels and a PER-SEED pairing of two ladders that share
     their seeds; the shear edge comes from a different campaign, N and
     protocol, so it can have neither. The honest statement is that the width
     trend is not established, not that the shipped one is refuted.
   SETTLED: the traverse survives all three readings at 2.20-2.45 windows per unit CV (endpoint statistic), so
     'a CV ladder of about 0.4 moves a material across the
     window' does not depend on the edge definition.
   NOT SETTLED: the critical-state compressibility. Reading C uses the
     isotropic proxy. The 4-pressure constant-pressure ladder measures it
     directly and would also give phi_c(p -> 0) with no proxy at all.
   NOT SETTLED: phi_0 itself at CV 0.5, whose THIN 3-rung ladder dominates
     every error bar above. A frictionless constant-pressure ladder would
     give phi_0 the same way phi_c now comes.
