**WHAT THIS GATE CAN AND CANNOT DECIDE** (referee, 2026-09-18). It calls `shape.sub_sphere_diameter` on BOTH sides of the comparison -- the construction used it to size the members, and the prediction uses it again -- and `density_scale` is DEFINED as the reciprocal of the same quantity. #### THE CONTACT-STIFFNESS CONFOUND, as a PREDICTION that can fail

Yade's contact law never sees `d_eq`; it sees the MEMBER radius `ds/2`, and `ds/d_eq` falls 1.0000 -> 0.7937 along this ladder, so the normal stiffness drops with alpha at fixed `p_hat` (referee, 2026-09-18). Because `ds/d_eq` is a CONSTANT within a rung, the effect is exactly a rescaling of the pressure axis, and for `phi = phi_J + c*p_hat` a rescaling `p_hat -> k*p_hat` changes `c -> c/k` and leaves the INTERCEPT untouched. That argument makes a number: the fitted slope should rise by `d_eq/ds` from the lowest alpha to the highest.

| lognormal | 16.3579 | 12.2386 | 0.7482 | 1.2599 | 0.594 | 1.0751 | 0.696 |
| uniform | 15.8604 | 8.9017 | 0.5613 | 1.2599 | 0.445 | 1.0751 | 0.522 |

Contact multiplicity `m` = mean `n_contacts_raw/n_contacts` per rung (S4): alpha 1.00 -> 1.0000, alpha 2.00 -> 1.1719.

A measured ratio near the prediction says the stiffness change is a pure rescaling and the intercept is safe. **A ratio far from it does NOT invalidate phi_J by itself** -- elongated grains have a genuinely different compressibility, so the slope carries physics as well as the artefact, and this test cannot separate them. It is a NECESSARY condition for the rescaling argument, not a sufficient one, and it is reported as such.

**The SIGN of the miss** (referee, 2026-09-16): the member-radius argument predicts the slope RISES (1.2599); it falls in 3 of 3 families. Contact multiplicity pushes the other way (parallel springs stiffen the pair), and the two together predict 1.0751 -- the measured ratio is below THAT too in 3 of 3 families, by a factor of 0.432-0.696. So the multiplicity confound has the right sign and the wrong size, and the remainder is either the compressibility of an elongated packing or a contact effect this bookkeeping does not model; neither is separated here, and the rescaling argument stays uncertified.
