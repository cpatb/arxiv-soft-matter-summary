=== R2  the phi(I) dilatancy coefficient  🔴 RETRACTED ===
  Fitting phi against I gave a slope of 10-36 against a literature scale of ~0.2.
  Two orders out is a construction error, not a material difference: gdot is
  FIXED along a ladder, so 'phi(I)' is phi against PRESSURE in disguise -- a
  compression curve, not a rate-dependent dilatancy law -- and the top rungs are
  soft enough that the reported phi double-counts contact overlaps.
  The same error had made analyse_phase8's E1 the PRIMARY phi_c estimator. Both
  are retracted; E2, the vanishing-pressure root, is primary now.

=== R3  the early transient  🔴 RETRACTED as an elastic ramp; reported as a relaxation ===
  Intended reading: dp/dgamma = K' alpha (phi - phi_c), which would have tested
  the derivation's assumption that alpha does not move with CV. Measured,
  dp/dgamma is NEGATIVE on every cell -- including cells well above phi_c -- and
  the three-window gate FAILS on most. The model was wrong for this protocol:
  shear starts from an ISOTROPICALLY COMPRESSED state, not from the critical
  state, so the early transient is that preparation decaying away.
  ⚠ The alpha assumption therefore REMAINS UNTESTED. It needs constant-PRESSURE
  shear, which this protocol does not do.
