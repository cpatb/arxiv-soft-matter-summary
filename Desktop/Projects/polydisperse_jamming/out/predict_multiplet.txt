=== WHAT EACH BLOCK CAN AND CANNOT DECIDE ===
   A and B test the closure's TRANSFER to a PSD family no point of which is in
   its calibration set (16 binary compositions at size ratios 2 and 3). They
   cannot test Lambda: the DEM packs effective spheres, so it never sees the
   solid/excluded volume distinction Lambda is built from.
   C tests whether CV suffices. It cannot distinguish 'the closure is right'
   from 'some other non-CV descriptor is doing the work' -- only that a
   CV-only description is incomplete along the aggregation axis.
   NOTHING here tests assumption A1 (the effective-sphere replacement). That
   needs CLUMP cells at the same n_k, and it is registered separately.
