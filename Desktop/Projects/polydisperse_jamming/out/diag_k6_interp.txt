=== 🔴 USING THE ANCHOR AS A *FILTER* IS WITHDRAWN (referee, pass 3) ===
   Restricting to the 3 anchor-admissible forms would give a K = 6
   signal of 3.128 to 3.253 (K = 7 2.123 to 2.334) -- i.e. it would REMOVE the
   low end of the spread and make K = 6 look settled. That number is printed and
   NOT USED, for three reasons the file's own tables supply:

   (1) u = 1 is 0.200 from the nearest datum (u = 0.800), which is LARGER than the
       whole fitted span (0.133). Judging an INTERPOLANT by its behaviour that far
       outside its data tests extrapolation, and every form here is used STRICTLY
       INSIDE the data range (u6 = 0.758, u7 = 0.794 both lie in [0.667, 0.800]).
   (2) The IN-DOMAIN cross-validation two sections above ranks the forms the
       OPPOSITE way. Held-out middle point: the document's log-log form misses by
       9.4 %, the anchor-admissible power law by 18.6 %. The filter DISCARDS the
       best in-domain interpolant and KEEPS a worse one.
   (3) The admissible family is itself self-inconsistent on the data: its own
       pairwise exponents predict the third point wrong by up to -49.0 %.
   So s(u=1) = 0 is a true statement about the PHYSICS that is not a valid
   selection rule for an interpolant. It selects for a property ANTI-CORRELATED
   with fitting the measurements, and it happened to favour the answer I wanted,
   which is the direction to distrust. THE UNFILTERED SPREAD IS WHAT IS USED.

=== WHAT CAN DECIDE K = 6, AND WHY IT IS NOT A DENSER GRID ===
   The document asks for the ceiling to be recomputed at r = 4^(1/5) = 1.31951
   at several assumed truths before any prereg. That is necessary and NOT
   SUFFICIENT: identifiability here is a genuinely TWO-DIMENSIONAL, NON-MONOTONE
   criterion in (a, b), and the section above shows it cannot be collapsed into a
   one-dimensional threshold on the signal at all -- a non-resolvable truth can
   carry a LARGER signal than a resolvable one. So no assumed-truth grid, however
   dense, produces a bar. Only a REAL BLOCK at that ratio can, because it
   MEASURES the signal instead of interpolating it AND evaluates identifiability
   at the truth that actually obtains instead of scanning for one. Nothing above
   is a substitute for that, and nothing above moves a bar.
