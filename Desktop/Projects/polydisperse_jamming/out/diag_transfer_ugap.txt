   discretises into 160 equal-count classes and ADJACENT classes of a
   continuous PSD are nearly the same size. So the new knot does not cover the
   families' weight -- it only changes how PCHIP GUESSES on [u*, 1], which is
   where that weight lives. That is the mechanism behind section 2's verdict,
   and it says the family transfer needs a(u) at u > 0.9 -- precisely where the
   U-GAP block showed a binary composition curve has no signal left to fit.
