  !! CIRCULARITY WARNING, and it governs the verdict. phi_J_lin is the
     INTERCEPT OF A LINEAR FIT, i.e. it already assumes b = 1. Pinning
     phi_J to it and recovering b ~ 1 is therefore NOT independent
     evidence for the harmonic law, and must not be reported as a PASS.
     What the constrained fit does show is the SIZE of the trade-off.

  => VERDICT: THE EXPONENT IS UNDETERMINED BY THIS DESIGN, which is
     different from either passing or failing. Three facts together:
       (1) phi_J and b are strongly degenerate, corr = +0.675,
           p = 0.00056;
       (2) the phi_J differences driving the b shift are TINY --
           mostly 0.003-0.015, a few seed scatters -- yet b moves by
           0.58, so the fit is ill-conditioned, not merely noisy;
       (3) pinning phi_J to a b=1-assuming estimate returns b ~ 1,
           which is what circularity predicts and cannot confirm.
     The honest statement is that this p-ladder cannot determine
     phi_J and b simultaneously. The original FAIL should be restated
     as NOT MEASURABLE rather than as evidence against the contact
     law -- and equally, nothing here vindicates the contact law.
     Settling it needs a phi_J from an INDEPENDENT observable (e.g.
     an isostatic contact criterion), not from the same p-ladder.
