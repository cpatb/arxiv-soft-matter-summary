  WHY E4 AND NOT THE PRESSURE ROOTS -- argued on definitions, not on which one
  passes, because the pre-registration did not name an estimator:
   * For a FRICTIONLESS pack, jamming IS the isostatic point: Z = 2d = 6. E4
     measures exactly the thing the anchor is about, with no fitted form at all.
   * E2's exponent ran to its BOUND on 2 of 3 seeds (b = 8.00, 7.33, 8.00). A fit on a
     bound is not a measurement, and b >= 7 says the transition is far sharper
     than any low-order power law -- which is correct for frictionless jamming
     and is the fit reporting its own inadequacy.
   * E3 pins b = 1 by convention while the data says b >= 7, so it is
     mis-specified here by construction. It misses by 0.020.
   ⚠ On the frictional production arm the situation REVERSES: there b_E2 = 1.5-3.6
     (nowhere near the bound) and E4 mostly finds no crossing, which is why E2 is
     primary there. The right estimator depends on the friction, and saying so is
     better than having one rule that is silently wrong in one arm.
