=== READING ===
   The two protocols are compared on Delta phi_c, in each protocol's OWN units:
   CV 0.2: shear measured +0.0109, TAB predicts +0.0095 -- the model gets 87 % of the measured shift
   CV 0.5: shear measured +0.0533, TAB predicts +0.0398 -- the model gets 75 % of the measured shift
   CV 0.2: ISOTROPIC measured +0.0144, TAB predicts +0.0095 -- 66 % of the measured shift
   CV 0.5: ISOTROPIC measured +0.0543, TAB predicts +0.0399 -- 73 % of the measured shift

   A model that captures a similar FRACTION of the shift on both protocols has
   a shape that transfers and a magnitude that is wrong on both; a model that
   captures very different fractions has a protocol dependence in its shape.
   Which of those it is, is read off the two lines above -- not asserted here.

=== HOW SENSITIVE IS THE PREDICTION TO beta AT ALL? ===
   The whole transfer turns on re-basing the closure from beta_iso to
   beta_shear. If the PREDICTED Delta phi barely moves when beta does, then the
   re-basing is not what is being tested and saying 'the closure transfers'
   would credit it for something it did not do. Measured, not assumed:
      CV   d_pred(beta_iso)  d_pred(beta_shear)     change   as a fraction of the shear measurement se
     0.2          +0.009503           +0.009492  -0.000011                                       0.02
     0.5          +0.039860           +0.039838  -0.000023                                       0.01
   d(Delta_pred)/d(beta) = +0.003 and +0.006: the predicted
   shift is nearly INDEPENDENT of beta, so changing beta by the whole
   0.0036 between protocols moves the prediction by ~1e-4, which is
   a few per cent of the measurement's own standard error.

   CONSEQUENCE, and it weakens the reading above: this comparison CANNOT FAIL
   through the re-basing, because the re-basing does essentially nothing. What
   it actually compares is ONE fixed model prediction against TWO different
   measurements. So the honest statement is NOT 'the closure transfers across
   protocols' -- it is:
     (1) the closure's predicted Delta phi is protocol-BLIND to 1e-4;
     (2) the two protocols' MEASURED Delta phi differ from each other (isotropic
         +0.0144 vs shear +0.0109 at CV 0.2), and the single prediction sits
         between them;
     (3) it under-predicts BOTH, by 13-34 %, which is the same direction and a
         similar size to the ternary's non-additivity residual.
   A test of whether the SHAPE is protocol-dependent would have to move
   something the prediction is actually sensitive to, and beta is not it.
