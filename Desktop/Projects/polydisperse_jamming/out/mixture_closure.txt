=== what is NOT established ===
   * The calibration has size ratios 2 and 3 only. A continuous PSD is a sum over
     many pairs, most of them at ratios BELOW 2, where nothing constrains the
     exponents and where the binary campaign already showed the textbook form is
     worst. A ratio ladder (1.25, 1.5, 5, 10) is what would fix that, and it is
     the same runner with a different --ratio.
   * beta is the same measured number in every column, so none of this tests the
     model at a different friction; section 7 of mixture_model.py does, at the
     textbook exponents only.
   * Every phi here is read at ONE pressure rung. Nothing says the interaction
     functions are pressure-independent, and this campaign cannot say it.
